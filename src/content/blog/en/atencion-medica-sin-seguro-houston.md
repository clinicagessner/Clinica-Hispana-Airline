# Healthcare Without Insurance in Houston: Your Real Options

**Having no insurance does not mean having no care.** In Houston there are at least three routes: community health centers with income-based fees, Harris County programs, and self-pay clinics like ours, where you pay for the service and that is the whole transaction.

Texas has **the highest uninsured rate in the country**, according to [KFF](https://www.kff.org/state-category/health-coverage-uninsured/). Around here that is not a statistic — it is half the neighborhood.

## What options actually exist?

### Federally qualified health centers (FQHC)

They charge on a sliding scale based on your income, which can bring a visit down to very little. You need proof of income, and appointments are often weeks out. You can find the nearest one through the [official HRSA locator](https://findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov/).

### Harris County programs

[Harris Health](https://www.harrishealth.org/) offers financial assistance to county residents who meet the income requirements. The process takes time and paperwork, but if your situation is stable and you will need ongoing care, it is worth starting.

### Self-pay clinics

You pay for the service, with no insurance in the middle and no income verification. That is what we do: you know the price before the visit, no appointment, no paperwork.

None of the three is better in the abstract. An FQHC is unbeatable if your income is low and you can wait. A self-pay clinic wins when you need to be seen today.

## Clinic or emergency room?

This is the decision that costs uninsured families the most money.

**Go to the ER, no hesitation:** chest pain, trouble breathing, weakness on one side of the body, bleeding that will not stop, loss of consciousness.

**Go to the clinic:** flu and cough, urinary infections, sore throat, minor wounds, diabetes or blood pressure management, lab work, physical exams, vaccines.

An emergency room will treat a throat infection, yes. But that uninsured visit can run into hundreds or thousands of dollars for something a clinic resolves for a fraction of it. The wait is usually worse too, because the ER treats by severity, not by arrival time.

## How does self-pay work here?

No insurance, no income verification, no appointment. **Ask the price before your visit** — we will tell you straight, and we take cash, debit and credit.

What we also do is tell you what you need and what you do not. If you come in for a test and we think half of what you were told to get is unnecessary, we will say so.

## How to spend less without neglecting yourself

**Get ahead of it.** Managed diabetes costs lab work every three months. Unmanaged diabetes costs hospital stays. The [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/) is clear that consistent control prevents damage to kidneys, eyes and nerves — and that damage is what truly ruins an uninsured family.

**Do not wait for pain.** Most of what we treat would have been cheaper and simpler two weeks earlier.

**Always ask the price.** At any clinic, including this one. If nobody gives you a straight answer, walk away.
